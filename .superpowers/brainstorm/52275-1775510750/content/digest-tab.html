Key Behaviors

  • Video/Audio toggle — user picks their mode. Preference is remembered.
  • Mini-player persists — when switching to Stories or Archive tabs, audio keeps playing with a mini-player bar above the tab bar.
  • Past digests — scrollable list of previous days. Each plays the YouTube video or Archive.org audio.
  • Lock screen controls — standard iOS Now Playing integration via MPNowPlayingInfoCenter.
  • Offline — previously played audio is cached. Video requires connectivity.
