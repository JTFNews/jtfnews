Tab Descriptions

1

Stories

Today's verified facts. Each card shows: the fact, source badges with accuracy ratings, ownership disclosure, and time. Corrections are inline with visual distinction. Pull to refresh.

2

Digest

Daily audio/video. Embedded YouTube player for the daily digest video. Below it, an audio-only option (Archive.org podcast). Past digests listed chronologically. Play controls persist as a mini-player when switching tabs.

3

Archive

The full record. Calendar or date-picker to browse past days. Full-text search across all archived stories. Each archived day shows its stories + link to that day's digest. Downloaded archives are cached for offline search.
